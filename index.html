Macro Overview

Background & Governance

Dholera SIR (Special Investment Region) is India's pioneer greenfield smart industrial city. Strategically located in Gujarat and spanning a massive 920 Sq. Km, it features a planned developable area of 422.42 Sq. Km across residential, commercial, industrial, and IT zones.

DICDL Governance (SPV)

Managed by Dholera Industrial City Development Ltd. (DICDL), a unique collaboration of the Central Government (49%) & State Government (51%), ensuring focused development, safety, and security.

World-Class Planning Partners

Designed to meet global standards. The master plan was crafted by Halcrow UK, with project management by AECOM USA, and construction execution by L&T.

Town Planning Scheme

Master Plan & Phased Stages

Dholera SIR is developed under detailed Town Planning (TP) Schemes. The developable area covers 422.42 Sq. Km divided into six major Town Planning Schemes.

Phase 1: Activation Area

Covers 22.54 Sq. Km (combining TP 2A and TP 4A). Fully developed with physical infrastructure including roads, sewer networks, and water lines.

Phase 2 & Phase 3

Phase-2 development covers 137 Sq. Km (target by Y2032) and Phase-3 completes the rest (target by 2042).

Market Reality Warning for Investors

Inside Dholera SIR: Minimum plot size is 350 Sq. Yd. with minimum 40-ft internal roads under strict DGDCR guidelines. Backed by government infrastructure.

Outside Dholera SIR: Over 60+ townships exist 15–25 km away under Gram Panchayats. They lack smart utilities, are not RERA-conforming, and lack a master plan. "Inside Dholera SIR is comparable to Chandigarh, while outside is like Dera Bassi/Kharar."
